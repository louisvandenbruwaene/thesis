# Thesis and seminar slide review — 11 September 2026

Reviewed the active project in `Documents/Projects/thesis`, using the thesis
sources and appendix to check the seminar deck's formulas, explanations,
examples, references and proof-status labels. This was a consistency and
mathematical spot-checking review, not a line-by-line certification of every
appendix proof. No conjecture was promoted to an author-checked result.

## Corrections

- **Mader double count, slides:** the claim that every edge crosses two tree
  cuts was false. At most `n−1` edges have tree distance one; the others cross
  at least two cuts. The slide now includes the essential inequality
  `2|E|−(n−1) ≤ (m−1)(n−1)`. The multigraph slide now explains precisely why
  the distance-one bound fails with parallel copies.
- **Attainment ranges:** moved `n ≥ m` from the simple edge upper bound to
  its lower construction, added that range to the simple vertex lower bound,
  and described the spoke graph as almost regular, allowing the parity
  exception. Added `2 ≤ n ≤ m` to the thickened complete digraph construction
  and `n ≥ r` to the directed hypergraph constructions.
- **Small-order thesis statement:** replaced the contradictory wording about
  strict inequality when `n < m` with the exact complete-graph value and its
  sole equality exception `(n,m)=(2,3)` for `n ≥ 2`.
- **Augmented bipartite proof:** explained why a route from a fixed source
  cannot finish through another source's arc into the target. The cut has
  `m−1` arcs even though the target's total indegree can be larger.
- **Hypergraph cut proof, slides:** replaced the suggestion of cutting off a
  single hyperedge with the actual induction on traces across a cut of at
  most `m−1` copies. Distinguished the pictured star hypertree at `m=2` from
  the degree-capped hub construction for the general attainment range.
- **Directed hypergraph asymptotic:** the bounded-outdegree construction is
  only linear at fixed `m`. The slide now attributes asymptotic optimality to
  the separate bipartite construction. It also makes clear that choosing
  `m−1` outgoing copies is a sufficient construction rule, not a necessary
  outdegree bound on every feasible hypergraph.
- **Proof status:** changed the directed multigraph vertex construction's
  general lower-bound badge to PROPOSED, matching Appendix A.66 and its
  dependency A.63. Its independently enumerated three-vertex value keeps
  COMPUTED status. The legend now explains PROPOSED and accurately describes
  completed enumeration as establishing a finite optimum.
- **References:** corrected the directed splitting reference from A.54 to
  A.63, included the proposed bound A.66, changed the two-step bound reference
  from its definition A.28 to the counting lemma A.32, included the attainment
  theorem A.10, and removed the undirected slide's reference to a directed
  tree construction.
- **Notation and layout:** defined the search objective's connectivity symbol
  and penalty weight. Shortened the backup legend so the added status
  explanation fits without overflow.
