# Follow-up audit, 6 September 2026

This report supplements the 5 September review. Its treatment of the remaining directed conjecture supersedes that report. This is a substantial audit pass, not certification of every statement or archival script.

## Mathematical presentation

The former eventual-optimality conjecture is now a comparison of three explicit dense constructions. No matching upper bound is asserted. The clique-core example at n=12 and m=5 has 57 arcs and maximum local arc-connectivity 4. This disproves the old value 56 but does not prove that 57 is optimal. The refuted decomposition remains excluded from the live thesis. The research-note proof of eventual equality has not been adopted.

Proof revisions explain the fundamental-cut property needed from a Gomory-Hu tree, why a reachability skeleton preserves existence rather than counts of paths, how repeated vertices are removed from concatenated walks and why marking arcs for deletion gives an upper estimate. The incidence-rank argument now spells out its smaller-instance step. The matching arguments require greedy maximal families, not maximum-size families. AI-assistance badges remain in place.

## Code and evidence corrections

An integer-overflow bug made a graph with 4294967296 parallel arcs appear to have connectivity zero. Large capacities now use exact Python-integer flow when the accelerated paths cannot safely represent them. Canonical keys no longer truncate large multiplicities. Edge counts and degrees avoid sum overflow. Regression cases cover these failures.

Search deadline checks now occur inside previously unchecked loops. Budgets reject invalid numbers. These are cooperative stopping targets, not hard process limits. A feasibility check already underway can finish after the requested deadline.

The figure driver no longer lifts raw search values to construction counts, smooths them across orders or transfers values between models. Search, supplied constructions, independent enumeration and theorem bounds are separate evidence categories. Contradictory evidence raises an error rather than being silently capped. The 32 panel search and exact series were compared before and after the structural refactor and were unchanged. Construction series were deliberately improved where a verified construction was available.

The default figure workflow only renders current thesis assets. It does not rerun the archived experiment collection. Fresh machine records preserve witnesses, methods, budgets, elapsed times and environment information. Cache writes use atomic replacement. Conflicting completed exact values cannot be promoted silently.

The generating-search timing comparison previously compared different tasks as though they were interchangeable solvers. The thesis now distinguishes blind maximisation, construction-seeded maximisation and classification at a supplied target. The last task does not independently prove that the target is optimal. The corresponding speed-up claims were removed.

## Experiments and provenance

The historical machine_values.json was not modified. Four comparison tables and data/search_evidence.json expose 400 search and construction comparisons. Historical missing witnesses and elapsed times remain unknown. Budgets of 0.4 or 4 seconds for those searches are disclosed separately from enumeration budgets.

Seven fresh unaided tabu searches used seed zero and a two-second target per case. All reached their known optima. Their actual elapsed times, raw values and witnesses are stored in data/rediscovery.json. The supplied optimum is used only for the comparison table, not passed to search. All seven witnesses were checked for feasibility and star-shaped support. These short validation runs are not an isolated performance benchmark and are not one-hour experiments.
