\section{A gallery of extremal graphs}\label{app:gallery-extremal}

The extremal graphs are not abstractions. \Cref{fig:extremal-gallery} draws nine of the named families the body argues about, each at a deliberately large representative size rather than at the smallest case, so the structure is visible. These are the special, structured extremisers, not the trivial small trees. Two are directed hub constructions: the bidirected star, with arcs both ways to every leaf, and the directed double star, its multigraph analogue with hub arcs at full multiplicity $m-1$. The latter ties the doubled bipartite wall $2\cdot B_{3,4}$ at the crossover. The wall and the dense bidirected complete graph are separate non-hub directed families. Another panel is the undirected multigraph star, and the remaining families are star hypertrees, drawn as metro maps in which each hyperedge is one coloured line through its members. The program builds every displayed family directly. Their extremal status comes from the theorems and finite classifications cited in the body, not from the separate best-found gallery JSON.
