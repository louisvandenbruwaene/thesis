the preamble/shared styles and bibliography, the popularising summary,

## Substantive corrections

| Finding | Correction and reason |

| The independent two-step-budget checker assigned effectively infinite capacity to a direct arc in its vertex-split graph. | Each original arc has capacity one, so the direct route is counted once. Previously even one arc could be counted as several internally disjoint routes. |

## Mathematical and presentation consistency

- The checked directed multigraph arc theorem was still presented as conjectural
  in parts of the code and as purple in the thesis/slides variant tree. Those
  labels now agree with the checked theorem. Evaluating its construction still
  does **not** pretend to be a completed enumeration in the `solve` result.
- `K_3(n)=2(n-1)` follows from the checked hypergraph vertex upper bound at
  `r=2` and the thickened-tree lower bound. The synthesis, seminar deck, figure
  generator and value table now recognise this. The table's values 12 and 14
  retain theorem status, rather than acquiring a fictitious machine certificate.
- The directed multigraph vertex leading term follows from the checked general
  directed hypergraph bound at `r=2`, together with the thickened bipartite
  construction. The synthesis and seminar backup slide now identify that
  leading term as established. The separate proposed classification and
  block arguments retain their original status.
- Surface plotting no longer calls established directed `m=2` values,
  directed multigraph arc values, undirected multigraph vertex `m<=3` values,
  or the proved hypergraph attainment ranges open. It also uses the existing
  Sørensen–Thomassen helper for the simple undirected vertex case `m=5`.
- The block table now distinguishes computed block scores from exact whole-graph
  optima. Converting them into `K_m(n)` requires the proposed block reduction.
  In particular, the `m=4` discussion no longer presents the tested block range
  as an unconditional computation of all corresponding whole-graph optima.
- The nine-vertex block sweep covered 191826 two-connected graphs **with at
  least fourteen edges**. The appendix and slides now say this. The omitted
  supports cannot improve `g_6(9)=54`, since at most thirteen edges contribute
  at most `4*13=52` to that score. The score alone does not establish the
  whole-graph upper bound `K_6(9)<=54` without the proposed reduction.
- The seminar definitions now maximise over graphs of fixed order `n`.
  The flow explanation requires the maximum over endpoint pairs, and the
  splitting diagram exempts the two endpoints from the unit vertex capacity.
  The penalty description no longer asserts that infeasibility can never pay.
  One search illustration had seven vertices in a six-vertex example; it now
  has six, and its displayed objective specifies `m=3`.
- The asymptotic statements in the slides now state their necessary ranges:
  `c_m>m/2` for `m>=5`, and the positive linear correction to `n^2/4` for fixed
  `m>=3`. Hypergraph examples distinguish the `m=2` star hypertree from the
  degree-capped hub construction needed for the stated `m=3` attainment range.
- The seminar title slide now matches the thesis cover: Dr. Stijn Cambie is
  the promotor. This follows the cover's metadata; it is not an independent
  verification of anyone's current title or official role.
