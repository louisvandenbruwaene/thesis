{$k_m(n)=\ell_m(n)$ for $m\le4$, \ $k_5(n)=\floorfrac{8n}{3}-4$ for $n\ge14$ \ \sProved\newline{\scriptsize\color{black!50}$m\ge6$:} \sOpen}
{$c_m>m/2$ for $m\ge5$ \ \sProposed\ \ {\scriptsize\color{black!50}under the block formulation}}
{\begin{vlist}
  \item at $m=5$ the two agree to $n=13$, first gap $33$ against $32$ at $n=14$
  \item $c_m=\lim_n k_m(n)/(n-1)$, and $m/2<c_m\le2(m-1)$ for $m\ge5$
\end{vlist}}
{\cBlocks}{$2$-connected blocks at a cut vertex \ \tNear}
{Block sweeps reach small blocks only, and the recursive constructions in the

  \item the clique-core example disproves the earlier hub-versus-bipartite formula

{\cAugBip}{augmented bipartite digraph \ \tNear}
