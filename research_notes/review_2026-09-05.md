# Thesis revision, 5 September 2026

Implemented Stijn Cambie's feedback in the thesis sources and rebuilt `main.pdf`.

## Conjectures

- Removed the former decomposition conjecture A.28 and all live references to it. The superseded discussion is archived in `offcuts.tex`, explicitly marked as refuted.
- Conjecture 3.1 now asserts only eventual equality with `floor((n+m-2)^2/4)` for each fixed `m`, with an unspecified threshold. It makes no exact claim for smaller orders.
- Added the attributed clique-core construction, its separating cuts, arc count, counterexample range, and the necessary threshold `N(m) >= 3m-2` for `m >= 5`. The 57-arc example is a lower bound, not a claim that 57 is optimal.
- Read the supplied eventual-exactness argument. Following the requested concise treatment, it remains in `eventual_exactness_short.tex` rather than being incorporated as a new theorem. The thesis explains that the eventual statement remains conjectural pending the author's proof verification.
- Updated the contribution statement, summaries, status diagram, finite-value grids, captions and companion-program descriptions. Finite orders no longer carry a curve labelled as the refuted conjectured optimum. The crossover plot now starts at `n=m`, where its constructions exist.

## Other corrections

- Restricted the simple directed hypergraph leading-constant conclusion to `r >= 3`. At `r=2`, the simple-digraph leading constant differs from the multigraph one.
- Distinguished the general orientation model, which allows all tail/head splits, from an unproved claim about each separately prescribed tail-set size.
- Added the missing simplicity hypothesis to the smaller-half deletion lemma.
- Removed an invalid inference of individual degree-product bounds from a sum bound, and corrected the explanation of equality in the Cauchy–Schwarz estimate.
- Corrected the inference that a construction's constant improvement gives a constant upper bound on the improvement of the optimum.
- Excluded zero-multiplicity “complete blocks” from the directed construction and its figure generator. Corrected the positive-multiplicity endpoint in the undirected discussion.
- Clarified exact connectivity versus cut upper bounds, original-vertex interiors versus incidence edge-nodes, and the incidence-rank lemma's allowance for edges inside `X`.
- Corrected stale statements about induction, AI badges and computational dependence. Existing author verification badges were preserved.
- Corrected simple versus multigraph enumeration ranges, hyperedge gate capacities, and the description of temporary infeasibility during tabu search.
- Corrected the assertion that nauty's `directg` and `watercluster2` cannot generate bidirected edges. The official [nauty 2.9 guide](https://pallini.di.uniroma1.it/nug29.pdf) documents that capability. Added its bibliography entry.
- Updated and shortened the standalone popularising summary, including its distinction between eventual conjecture and proved leading term.
- Reset the ignored `revision.tex` marker to an unrecorded working revision. The previous `submitted-9` tag still identifies its original source snapshot.
